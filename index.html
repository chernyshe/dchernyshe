About me: I am a mathematician specializing in geometric analysis, working primarily at the intersection of non-linear partial differential equations and functional analysis. I completed my PhD at McGill University in May 2026 under the supervision of Professors Jérôme Vétois and Pengfei Guan. My research centers on weighted quasilinear critical elliptic equations and geometric problems exhibiting a loss of compactness. My most recent work involves questions of existence, multiplicity, and symmetry prescription for Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg problems. In my spare time, I am interested in network security and post-quantum cryptography.
